I build scientific software and computing infrastructure for demanding numerical simulations in physics and beyond.

## Current Work

I am a Research Software Engineer at VU Amsterdam and a developer and maintainer of [ADA](https://rdm.vu.nl/tools/ada/index.html), VU Amsterdam's HPC cluster for research computing.
Alongside this, I collaborate with researchers across various disciplines on scientific software, with a focus on making computational research robust, scalable, and maintainable.

## Interests

- Scientific computing and high-performance numerical simulations
- Computational condensed matter, mesoscopic physics, and interacting electron systems
- Algorithms for simulations near compute and memory limits

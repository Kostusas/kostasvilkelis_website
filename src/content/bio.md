I work at the intersection of scientific computing, computational physics, and research infrastructure. I am interested in building software and systems that make demanding numerical simulations reliable, scalable, and maintainable.

## Current Work

I am a Research Software Engineer at VU Amsterdam and a developer and maintainer of [ADA](https://rdm.vu.nl/tools/ada/index.html), VU Amsterdam's HPC cluster for research workloads. Alongside this, I collaborate with researchers across various disciplines on scientific software and simulations, with a focus on making computational research robust, scalable, and maintainable.

## Interests

- Scientific computing and numerical simulations
- High-performance computing
- Algorithms for large-scale models near compute and memory limits
- Computational condensed matter, mesoscopic physics & Interacting electron systems
